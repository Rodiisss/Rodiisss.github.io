Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-27

This site hosts a personal automation project ("Clipper") that records Twitch streams and publishes highlight clips to YouTube.

What this app does with your Google account

When you authorize the app, it uses your Google account to interact with YouTube. It requests two scopes:

  • youtube.upload — upload videos to your YouTube channel.
  • youtube.readonly — read your YouTube account.

The app uses these permissions only to upload the clips it produces.

Data we collect

No personal data is collected beyond the OAuth authorization itself (the YouTube account credentials described above). There is no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party services.
